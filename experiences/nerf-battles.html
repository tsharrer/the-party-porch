Our flagship experience: a full mobile Nerf battlefield delivered right to your backyard, park or venue.

We bring the blasters, hundreds of darts, safety glasses and inflatable bunkers to build an epic battlefield wherever you are. Add a referee and we'll run safe, high-energy games from start to finish so you can just enjoy the party.

From $180 for up to 12 players (~2 hours). Referee +$100.

What's included

  • Blasters & hundreds of darts for the whole crew
  • Safety glasses for every player
  • Inflatable bunkers & battlefield obstacles
  • Delivery & pickup included (setup not included - add setup + tear down for +$40)
  • Optional trained referee to run every game

How your Nerf party works

  • 1. Book online - pick your date, player count, and whether you want a referee. Get an instant price estimate.
  • 2. We deliver - we drop off blasters, hundreds of darts, safety glasses and inflatable bunkers right to your backyard or park. Set it up yourself, or add setup + tear down for +$40.
  • 3. Battle on! - set it up and play, or let our referee run safe, high-energy games from start to finish.
  • 4. We pack it up - when the fun's over, we come back to collect the gear. No cleanup for you.

Space needed: a flat backyard, park or open area about half a basketball court suits 10–20 players. Not sure? Send us a photo or your address and we'll confirm the best fit.
