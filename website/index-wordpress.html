Good to know

Nerf party FAQ

How much is a Nerf party in Houston?

Our mobile Nerf battle party rental starts at $180 for up to 12 players and about 2 hours. That includes delivery, blasters, darts, safety glasses and battlefield obstacles. Add a referee for +$100 to have our host run every game and handle safety.

What ages is this for?

Nerf battles are great for ages 5 and up — birthdays, team parties, church & community events, even adults. We adjust game intensity to the group.

How much space do we need?

A flat backyard, park, or open area about the size of half a basketball court works great for 10–20 players. Not sure? Send us a photo or your address and we'll let you know.

Do you provide safety glasses?

Yes — safety glasses are included and required for every player. Eye protection is non-negotiable at every battle.

What if it rains?

No problem — reschedule with 48 hours' notice and your deposit transfers to your new date.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 3–4 weeks ahead to secure your preferred date, especially for weekend parties in spring and fall when dates fill fast. Need something sooner? Ask us — we'll do our best.

How do I reserve my date?

Submit the booking form above. We'll confirm availability and send a simple invoice — a $50 deposit locks in your date, with the balance due the day of the party.
