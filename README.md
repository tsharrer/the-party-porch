## 🎯 The model in one paragraph
Nerf wars are the **hero product** that gets you booked and in the driveway. Every booking
is then an opportunity to add higher-margin lines — photo booths, snack machines,
backdrops — raising average order value. Same van, same route, same customer. Reinvest
profit into more kits and hired crews until the business runs weekends without you on-site.

## 🚦 Current phase
**Pre-launch / Foundation (July 2026).** See [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md) for live status.

## 🧭 Guiding principles
1. **Content is the marketing engine** — film every party, post 4–5×/week.
2. **Raise the average ticket** before chasing more parties (bundles > volume).
3. **Let demand pull products in** — don't stockpile gear.
4. **Hire before burnout** — bring on help in Phase 2, not after you're maxed.
5. **Safety + professionalism** — waivers, eye protection, on-time, clean setup.
