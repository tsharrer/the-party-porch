# First Clients Playbook

Getting the first 3 bookings is the hardest part. Here's the exact sequence.

---

## Stage 1: The First 3 (free / founding) — before you have any proof
You need footage and reviews more than money right now. Trade parties for content.

1. **Family & friends with kids** — offer a FREE or $99 pilot party. Your only ask: let you
   film it and get a written/video review. Target 2 of these.
2. **Post in your personal network** — "I'm launching a Nerf war party business! Booking 3
   founding families at 50% off in exchange for honest feedback + a review. Who's in?"
3. **Buy-Nothing / mom groups** — offer 1 heavily discounted "founding" party.

**Deliverable from each:** 5+ video clips, 3+ photos, 1 review, 1 testimonial quote.

---

## Stage 2: The First 10 (paid, discounted) — Founding Family offer
- Offer the **first 5–10 paid parties with the referee FREE** (rental + our host for the
  rental price, e.g. $180 instead of $280 at 12 players) labeled "Founding Family — Fall 2026."
- Promote in: local FB parent groups, Nextdoor, Marketplace, your Stories with a countdown.
- Every one of these = more reviews and footage → makes full-price sales easy.

---

## Stage 3: Full-price flywheel
Once you have ~10 reviews + a content bank, the flywheel spins: content → inquiry → booking
→ more content + review → more inquiries. Focus shifts to referrals, repeat, and partnerships.

---

## The inquiry → booking conversion flow
1. **Respond fast** (< 1 hour). Speed wins party bookings.
2. **Ask 4 questions:** date, child's age, # of kids, location (to confirm you serve the area).
3. **Quote the rental, then upsell the referee** + one add-on (anchor on the full experience;
   families who want to run it themselves keep the rental).
4. **Send the booking link / form** + a photo or 15-sec video so they can visualize it.
5. **Secure with a deposit** ($50–100 non-refundable) via Stripe/Square/Venmo — this is the
   difference between an inquiry and a booking. No deposit = not booked.
6. **Confirm details** 3 days before (address, setup space, parking, headcount).
7. **Day-after:** thank-you text + Google review link + referral offer.

## Simple liability waiver (have every host sign at booking — get a lawyer to review before scale)
> I understand Nerf/foam-dart activities involve running and foam projectiles and carry
> inherent risks. Eye protection is provided and required. I confirm participants will follow
> all safety rules given by The Party Porch staff. I release The Party Porch from liability
> for minor injuries arising from normal participation, except in cases of gross negligence.
> Host name / signature / date. (⚠️ Replace with an attorney-reviewed waiver + get event
> liability insurance before public/large events.)

---

## Safety rules (state before every battle)
1. Eye protection ON at all times during play.
2. No point-blank/face shots; aim for the body.
3. No pushing/tackling.
4. Only our darts and blasters.
5. When the host calls "GAME OVER," blasters down.
Well-run safety = happy parents = referrals.

## Target booking milestones
- End of Aug 2026: 3 completed (founding) parties
- End of Sep 2026: 3+ paid parties, first full-price booking
- End of Dec 2026: 6+ bookings/month consistently
- Track every inquiry + source in your tracker so you learn what channel works.
