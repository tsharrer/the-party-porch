## Sourcing tips
- Buy blasters in **used lots** — people offload their kids' collections cheap.
- Standardize on **one dart type** so refills/counting are simple.
- Count darts after every party (you WILL lose some in grass — bring extras).
- Start DIY on bunkers; upgrade to inflatables once cash flows.

## First reinvestment (Phase 1)
- **Popcorn machine (~$200)** → launch **The Snack Bar**, a +$100/party add-on at ~90% margin.
  Payback in ~2 parties. Best early ROI upgrade.
