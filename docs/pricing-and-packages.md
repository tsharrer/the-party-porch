# Pricing & Packages (Houston — undercut strategy)

**Positioning:** Fully-hosted Nerf war parties (we set up, run every game, clean up) priced
**below** Houston's hosted competitors. Penetration pricing to win reviews + market share now,
then step up toward market rate ($349–$400) in 2027 as the brand builds.

## Founding Family launch offer (Aug–Sep 2026)
First 5–10 paid parties at **$149** in exchange for a review + permission to film.

## Pricing strategy notes
- **Why undercut:** you're new with no reviews. Being the clear value pick gets the first
  wave of bookings + footage fast. Reviews are the asset that lets you raise prices later.
- **Step-up plan:** once you have ~20 five-star reviews and steady demand (Phase 2, 2027),
  raise Standard to $299 → $329 → market ($349–400). Grandfather repeat customers.
- **Protect margin now** via add-ons/bundles (Snack Bar is ~90% margin) rather than racing
  competitors to the bottom on the base price.

## Targets
- Launch average ticket: **~$330** → **~$500** with bundles.
- Net profit/party at launch: ~$180–260 (rises as prices normalize toward market).
