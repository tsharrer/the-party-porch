# Marketing Plan

Your marketing is a **content engine + local trust**, not a big ad budget. Nerf battles are
inherently filmable — kids laughing, slow-mo dart hits, team wins. Lean into that.

---

## 1. Content Engine (your #1 channel — free)
**Rule: film every party. Post 4–5 short videos/week on TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts.**

### Winning content formats (proven for party/rental accounts)
1. **Kid reactions** — the moment they see the battlefield set up. Pure gold.
2. **Slow-mo action** — dart hits, dives behind bunkers, victory celebrations.
3. **Setup timelapse** — empty backyard → epic battle arena in 60 seconds.
4. **"POV: it's your birthday and The Party Porch just pulled up."**
5. **Before/after** transformations.
6. **Parent testimonials** — 10-sec clips, "best party we ever threw."
7. **Behind-the-scenes** — packing the van, loading darts, "day in the life."
8. **Themed teasers** — Halloween zombie battle, glow-in-the-dark night battle.
9. **Educational** — "3 reasons Nerf parties beat the usual birthday."
10. **Trends/audio** — ride trending sounds with your party clips.

### Content rules
- Hook in the first 1 second (motion, a face, or text like "This backyard became a warzone").
- Always end with a soft CTA: "Booking Fall parties — link in bio."
- Post consistently > post perfectly. Batch-film at each party, cut into 5+ clips.
- Reuse: 1 party = a Reel + a TikTok + 3 Stories + a YouTube Short + a Facebook post.

---

## 2. Local Organic (free, high-intent)
- **Facebook groups:** join 15–20 local mom/parent/community/buy-nothing/birthday groups.
  Provide value, then post your offer where allowed. This is where birthday parents live.
- **Nextdoor:** post as a local small business; neighbors love supporting local.
- **Facebook Marketplace:** list "Nerf War Birthday Parties" as a service (renews weekly).
- **Google Business Profile:** critical for "party rentals near me" searches. Get reviews —
  aim for 10 reviews in the first 60 days. Post photo updates weekly.
- **School/PTA + church bulletins:** ask to be listed; offer a fundraiser night.

---

## 3. Referral & Repeat Engine
- **Referral offer:** "Refer a friend who books — you BOTH get $30 off." Put it on every invoice.
- **Repeat discount:** returning families get 10% off (birthdays repeat yearly!).
- **Party favors with your handle:** small card in each kid's goody bag: "Loved it? Have YOUR
  party with us → @yourpartyporch." Parents see it.
- **Google review ask:** text a review link the day after every party (script in playbook).

---

## 4. Partnerships (multiplies reach)
- **Venues & parks** that don't do entertainment — become their recommended party vendor.
- **Complementary vendors:** balloon artists, bakeries, face painters, bounce-house
  companies (that don't do Nerf) — cross-refer.
- **Micro-influencer moms** (2k–20k local followers): free/discounted party for authentic posts.
- **Schools/camps/churches:** field days, fall festivals, youth-group events (weekday revenue).
- **Corporate:** family fun days, team-building foam battles (higher tickets).

---

## 5. Paid Ads (start small, only after content proves out)
- Don't run ads until you have 3–4 videos that already perform organically. Boost the winners.
- **Facebook/Instagram local ads:** $5–10/day, radius-targeted to parents of 5–14 yr olds.
- **Objective:** message/lead form or link to booking. Track cost-per-booking.
- Scale spend only while cost-per-booking stays well under net profit per party (~$300).
- **Google Local Services / Search** later for "nerf party near me" high-intent clicks.

---

## 6. Channel priority by phase
| Phase | Primary | Secondary |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Pre-launch (Jul–Aug) | Content bank + FB groups + Google Business | Nextdoor, Marketplace |
| Launch (Sep) | Content daily + giveaway + reviews | Small boosted ad |
| Phase 1 (Oct–Dec) | Referrals + reviews + content | Venue partnerships |
| Phase 2+ (2027) | Paid ads + partnerships + SEO | Corporate/school outreach |

---

## 7. Marketing KPIs
- Followers + weekly reach/views
- Bookings/month + booking source (ask "how'd you hear about us?")
- Cost per booking (once running ads)
- Google reviews count + rating
- Referral rate + repeat rate
- Content cadence (target 4–5 posts/week)

## 8. Brand voice
Fun, energetic, family-friendly, reliable. You're the parent-approved company that makes
mom & dad look like heroes and gives kids the best party of the year. Emojis okay, but always
professional in DMs and on-time in person — reputation is everything in a referral business.
