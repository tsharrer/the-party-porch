owner's subsequent setup reports. This covers the conversation's work across
multiple dates; the filename is the compilation date, not every change's date.
Customer identities, addresses, phone numbers, credentials and payment URLs are

## Deposit invoices and temporary holds

Checkout sessions: booking email and browser redirect created different sessions.
Checkout links were not Dashboard invoices and could not be extended to seven days.

The owner selected a seven-day deposit hold, capped at event start. Implemented
one hosted invoice per stable booking ID, idempotent creation and a read-only
frontend payment lookup with polling and explicit errors. Paid reservations
remain; overdue unpaid invoices are voided before releasing inventory. Stripe
failures fail closed. Quotes and legacy/manual entries are not silently migrated.

Resolved trigger authorization through the explicit script-management scope and
installer authorization. The owner installed/deployed the backend, and matching
website changes were published.

An authorized live test verified an invoice link/deadline and inventory hold
without a card charge or customer SMS. Cleanup remains unconfirmed. A separate
private one-time replacement-invoice action was prepared and mock-tested; its
execution/send was not confirmed.

Customer email/text drafts and calendar drafts were prepared for manual bookings.
Opening a draft did not send a message or save a calendar entry. Existing
short-notice and overnight agreements must not be silently changed.

Evidence: `17909d8`; private session artifacts were not published.
