# Launch-Week Content Scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Format: **HOOK (first 1 second) → quick cuts → CTA.** 15–30 sec, vertical, on-screen
captions, trending audio. Film once, cut into a Reel + TikTok + Short + 3 Stories.

### 1 · The $600 Origin Story
> HOOK (on-screen): "I started a party business with $600. Here's the whole kit."
> CUTS: unbox blasters → pour 2,000 darts into bin → lay out safety glasses → load wagon.
> VO: "14 blasters, 2,000 darts, obstacles, and a plan to make it $10k/month."
> CTA: "Follow the journey. Booking fall parties now → @yourpartyporch"

### 2 · Backyard Transformation
> HOOK: "This normal backyard becomes a warzone in 60 seconds."
> CUTS: empty yard → timelapse building bunkers → finished arena → kids run in.
> CTA: "Want this in YOUR backyard? Link in bio."

### 3 · Kid Reaction (money shot)
> HOOK: "Watch his face when he sees his birthday setup."
> CUTS: kid rounds the corner → jaw drops → grabs a blaster → chaos + laughing.
> CTA: "Make your kid's jaw drop. Booking now."

### 4 · POV Pull-Up
> HOOK (on-screen): "POV: it's your birthday and The Party Porch just pulled up."
> CUTS: van door opens → gear rolls out → arena builds → battle montage.
> CTA: "We bring the party to you. @yourpartyporch"

### 5 · Slow-Mo Highlights
> HOOK: "Nerf hits in slow motion just hit different." (trending audio)
> CUTS: 4–5 slow-mo dart impacts, dives behind bunkers, a victory jump.
> CTA: "This could be your party. Fall dates open."

### 6 · Why It Beats a Normal Party
> HOOK: "3 reasons a Nerf war beats the usual birthday:"
> CAPTIONS: "1. Every kid is ACTIVE" → "2. Zero cleanup for you" → "3. We run the WHOLE thing."
> CTA: "Stress-free birthday? Link in bio."

### 7 · What's Included
> HOOK: "Here's exactly what you get when you book us."
> CUTS: blasters → darts → safety glasses → obstacles → "and a host who runs every game."
> CTA: "Rental $180, or add a referee to run it. DM 'BATTLE'."

### 8 · Founding Family Offer
> HOOK: "We're brand new — so our first 5 families get a deal."
> VO: "Founding Family deal: book the rental and we'll throw in the referee FREE. All I ask is an honest review."
> CTA: "Only 5 spots. Comment 'BATTLE' to claim one."

### 9 · Halloween Zombie Battle Teaser
> HOOK: "We turned a birthday into a zombie survival mission." 🧟
> CUTS: costumes → glow darts → foggy dusk battle → "survivors win."
> CTA: "Booking spooky-season battles now. @yourpartyporch"

### 10 · Giveaway / Launch Day
> HOOK: "We're giving away a FREE party to launch. Here's how to win:"
> VO: "Follow us, like this video, and tag 2 parents. Winner picked Friday."
> CTA: "Tag your party crew 👇 @yourpartyporch"

---
**Repurpose rule:** 1 film session → Reel + TikTok + YouTube Short + 3 Stories. Turn your
best-performing organic videos into boosted ads later.
