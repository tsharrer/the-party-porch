# Content Calendar

**Cadence:** 4–5 short videos/week (TikTok + IG Reels + YouTube Shorts). Batch-film at every
party, cut into 5+ clips. Hook in 1 second; end with "Booking Fall parties — link in bio."

## 15 ready-to-film video ideas
1. Kit reveal — "$600 turned my garage into a party business."
2. Setup timelapse — empty backyard → battle arena in 60 sec.
3. Kid reactions seeing the battlefield for the first time.
4. Slow-mo dart-hit montage set to trending audio.
5. "POV: it's your birthday and The Party Porch just pulled up."
6. Parent testimonial (10-sec clip).
7. "3 reasons a Nerf party beats the usual birthday."
8. Behind-the-scenes: packing the van / loading darts.
9. Team victory celebration + confetti moment.
10. Halloween "Zombie Survival" battle teaser (glow darts, costumes).
11. "How we run a party" run-of-show mini-vlog.
12. Blaster + gear tour ("what's included in your party").
13. Before/after backyard transformation.
14. Bloopers / funniest moments compilation.
15. Countdown/giveaway announcement.
