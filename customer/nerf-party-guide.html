Your Nerf Party Guide

Everything you need to set up the battlefield, run exciting games and have the equipment ready for pickup.

Setup Safety Game ideas Return checklist

Set up the battlefield

💧
Each inflatable needs one gallon of water before air is added. The water anchors the inflatable so it stays in place during play.

Choose the play area

Use a flat, open area away from streets, pools, grills, sharp objects and anything breakable. Clear away sticks, rocks and debris before laying out the inflatables.

Lay out the inflatables

Spread the obstacles around the field with enough room for players to move safely between them. Mix tall and short obstacles to create cover on both sides.

Add one gallon of water to each inflatable

Open the water-fill valve and add one full gallon of water to every inflatable. Close each water valve tightly before moving to the next step.

Add air with the provided pump

Connect the pump we provided to the air valve. Inflate each obstacle until it stands upright and feels firm, then close the air valve securely. Do not overinflate.

Create team bases and boundaries

Choose opposite ends of the field as team bases. Show every player the boundaries, the respawn areas and any spaces that are out of bounds.

Set up the gear station

Place blasters, darts and safety glasses together at the edge of the field. Give each player safety glasses before handing out blasters.

Rules before every game

Safety glasses stay on. Every player must wear the provided eye protection while a game is active.
Aim below the shoulders. No intentional shots at faces or heads.
Stay inside the boundaries. Stop play if anyone enters a street, driveway or other unsafe area.
Do not climb or tackle. Inflatables are for cover, not climbing, sitting or jumping on.
Call a pause when needed. Anyone may call “pause” for a fallen player, loose obstacle or safety issue.
Use adult supervision. An adult should explain the rules, start and stop each round, and watch the field throughout play.

Game ideas

🚩

Capture the Flag

Setup: Divide players into two teams. Place a flag or bright bandana at each team's base.

How to play: Steal the other team's flag and carry it back to your own base. When hit, return to your respawn point, count to 10 and rejoin. Players cannot guard within 10 feet of a flag.

Win: The first team to bring the opposing flag to its own base wins.

8–12 minute rounds
⭐

Protect the MVP

Setup: Each team secretly chooses one MVP. The MVP gets three lives; every other player respawns after a 10-count.

How to play: Protect your MVP while trying to hit the other team's MVP. The MVP raises a hand and counts down one life after each hit.

Win: The first team to take all three lives from the opposing MVP wins.

6–10 minute rounds
🎯

Team Elimination

Setup: Split into two teams at opposite ends of the field.

How to play: A player who is hit raises their blaster and leaves the field. No respawns during the round.

Win: The last team with at least one player still in the game wins.

3–5 minute rounds
🩹

Medic

Setup: Each team selects one medic.

How to play: When hit, a player kneels and raises a hand. The medic brings them back by safely tagging their shoulder. A medic who is hit returns to base, counts to 15 and then rejoins.

Win: Score one point for every opposing player hit. The team with the most points when time expires wins.

8 minute rounds
🧟

Zombie Outbreak

Setup: Choose one or two zombies. Everyone else begins as a survivor.

How to play: Zombies tag survivors with a hand; survivors may freeze a zombie for a 10-count with one dart hit. A tagged survivor becomes a zombie.

Win: Survivors win if anyone remains when time expires. Zombies win if everyone is converted.

5 minute rounds
👑

King of the Fort

Setup: Choose one inflatable as the fort and mark a nearby scoring zone. One team defends while the other attacks.

How to play: Attackers score by reaching the zone and holding it for five seconds. Hit players return to their base, count to 10 and rejoin.

Win: Attackers have five minutes to score three times, then teams switch roles.

5 minute turns

Return everything clean, dry and empty

Please complete every item before pickup. Moisture, water or dirt left in the equipment can damage it and delay the next party.

  • Collect all blasters, darts, safety glasses, inflatables and the pump.
  • Wipe dirt, grass, mud, food and spills from every item.
  • Open each air valve and fully deflate every inflatable.
  • Open each water valve and drain all water from every inflatable.
  • Tilt each inflatable in different directions until no water remains.
  • Wipe every item dry and allow damp surfaces to air-dry completely.
  • Close the valves only after the inflatables are empty and dry.
  • Place all equipment together in the pickup area.
